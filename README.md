## The entry

> I held 1% for three hours out of pure spite just to tell you: the power bank treats me better than you ever did.

112 characters, one line. The battery didn't die quietly — it held on at 1% for three hours out of pure spite, just to deliver the breakup line: the power bank treats it better. Jealousy, not a dying-battery complaint.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the entry to `entry.txt` inside `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `./out` when unset). Deterministic, no network, no dependencies.
